DRAGON BALL CHARACTER REFERENCE
A plain-text knowledge base covering characters from Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, and Dragon Ball Super.
Format note: each character has its own section. Every paragraph restates the character name so that
individual chunks remain self-contained after splitting.

=========================================================================
SECTION 1: MAIN PROTAGONISTS
=========================================================================

--- GOKU ---

Goku, also called Son Goku, is the main protagonist of the Dragon Ball series. He is a Saiyan whose
birth name is Kakarot. He was sent to Earth as an infant from Planet Vegeta and was found and raised
by an old martial artist named Grandpa Gohan, who named him Goku.

Goku suffered a head injury as a small child, which caused him to lose the aggressive instincts common
to Saiyan infants. This is the in-story explanation for why Goku grew up good-natured instead of
following his original mission to conquer Earth.

Goku trained under several teachers over the course of the series. He learned from Master Roshi at the
Turtle School, then from Korin, then from Kami and Mr. Popo, then from King Kai in the afterlife, and
later from Whis. His training arc is one of the longest running threads in the franchise.

Goku's signature technique is the Kamehameha, an energy wave channeled from cupped hands. He also uses
the Spirit Bomb, which gathers energy from living things and the environment, and Instant Transmission,
a teleportation technique he learned from the Yardrat people after the Namek arc.

Goku's transformations include Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan 2, Super Saiyan 3, Super Saiyan God, Super
Saiyan Blue, and Ultra Instinct. Goku first achieved Super Saiyan on Planet Namek during his fight with
Frieza, triggered by rage over Krillin's death.

Goku is married to Chi-Chi. They have two sons, Gohan and Goten. Goku is generally portrayed as
obsessed with fighting strong opponents and improving himself, sometimes at the expense of family
responsibilities, which is a recurring source of tension with Chi-Chi and Gohan.

--- VEGETA ---

Vegeta is the prince of the Saiyan race and the son of King Vegeta. He begins the Dragon Ball Z story
as an antagonist, arriving on Earth with Nappa to find the Dragon Balls and seek immortality. Over time
Vegeta becomes an ally and eventually one of Earth's primary defenders.

Vegeta's defining trait is his pride. Much of his character arc revolves around his rivalry with Goku
and his frustration at repeatedly falling behind a lower-class Saiyan. This pride drives both his worst
decisions and his eventual growth.

Vegeta's signature techniques include the Galick Gun, the Big Bang Attack, and the Final Flash. Final
Flash is most famously used during his fight against Perfect Cell.

Vegeta marries Bulma and has two children with her, Trunks and Bulla. His development from a genocidal
conqueror into a husband and father is one of the longest character arcs in the series.

Vegeta allowed himself to be possessed by the wizard Babidi during the Buu saga, becoming Majin Vegeta,
in order to regain his ruthless edge. He later sacrificed himself in a self-destruction attempt against
Majin Buu. In Dragon Ball Super, Vegeta develops Super Saiyan God, Super Saiyan Blue, and in the manga
continuity a form called Ultra Ego based on the power of destruction.

--- GOHAN ---

Gohan is the elder son of Goku and Chi-Chi, and the first half-Saiyan, half-human character introduced
in the series. He is named after Goku's adoptive grandfather.

Gohan is characterized as a scholar rather than a natural fighter. Chi-Chi pushes him toward academics,
and as an adult Gohan works as a researcher. Despite this, Gohan repeatedly displays enormous latent
power that surfaces under emotional stress.

Gohan was trained by Piccolo in the Saiyan saga, and the two develop a mentor and surrogate father
relationship that continues throughout the series.

Gohan achieved Super Saiyan 2 during the Cell Games and defeated Perfect Cell with a one-armed
Kamehameha, assisted by his father's spirit. This is often treated as the emotional climax of the Cell
arc.

Gohan later adopted a costumed crime-fighting persona called the Great Saiyaman. He unlocked a
potential-based power up from the Old Kai, commonly called his Ultimate or Mystic form. In the film
Dragon Ball Super: Super Hero he reaches a form called Beast.

Gohan marries Videl, the daughter of Mr. Satan, and they have a daughter named Pan.

--- PICCOLO ---

Piccolo is a Namekian and originally an antagonist. He is the offspring of King Piccolo, created
specifically to defeat Goku after King Piccolo's death.

Piccolo allied with Goku against Raditz at the start of Dragon Ball Z and died in that fight while
shielding Gohan. He was later revived with the Namekian Dragon Balls.

Piccolo trained Gohan and became his mentor and closest father figure. This relationship is central to
Piccolo's transformation from villain to hero.

Piccolo's signature attack is the Special Beam Cannon, known in Japanese as the Makankosappo, a
drilling energy beam fired from two fingers. He can also regenerate lost limbs, extend his arms, and
create clothing from his own energy.

Piccolo fused with Kami, restoring the original Namekian being they were split from, and later fused
with the Namekian warrior Nail on Planet Namek. In Dragon Ball Super: Super Hero, Piccolo gains a form
called Orange Piccolo.

SECTION 2: THE EARTH-BASED SUPPORTING CAST
=========================================================================

--- KRILLIN ---

Krillin is Goku's oldest and closest friend. He is a human martial artist who trained alongside Goku
under Master Roshi at the Turtle School after leaving the Orin Temple.

Krillin is frequently described as the strongest full-blooded human in the series, though he is
massively outclassed by the Saiyans and later villains.

Krillin's signature technique is the Destructo Disc, called the Kienzan in Japanese, a spinning blade
of energy capable of cutting through most defenses. He also uses the Solar Flare.

Krillin's death at Frieza's hands on Namek is the event that triggers Goku's first Super Saiyan
transformation. Krillin has died and been revived multiple times across the series.

Krillin marries Android 18 and they have a daughter named Marron. In later arcs Krillin works as a
police officer on Earth.

--- BULMA ---

Bulma is a scientist and inventor, and the second character introduced in the original Dragon Ball
manga. She meets Goku while searching for the Dragon Balls at the very start of the story.

Bulma is the daughter of Dr. Brief, founder of Capsule Corporation, the company that produces the
capsule technology used throughout the series to store vehicles and buildings in small containers.

Bulma invented the Dragon Radar, the device used to locate Dragon Balls, which drives the plot of many
arcs. She also repairs and reverse-engineers alien technology, including Saiyan scouters and space pods.

Bulma marries Vegeta and is the mother of Trunks and Bulla. Earlier in the series she dated Yamcha.

--- MASTER ROSHI ---

Master Roshi, also called the Turtle Hermit, is the martial arts teacher who trained Goku, Krillin,
Yamcha, and earlier Goku's adoptive grandfather and Ox-King.

Master Roshi is the inventor of the Kamehameha, which he reportedly spent fifty years developing.

Master Roshi has an extremely long lifespan attributed to drinking the Paradise Herb elixir. He is
portrayed as a skilled but comedic figure, frequently lecherous, which is a running gag in the series.

--- CHI-CHI ---

Chi-Chi is the daughter of the Ox-King and the wife of Goku. She met Goku as a child and later held him
to a promise of marriage that he had misunderstood.

Chi-Chi is the mother of Gohan and Goten. She is characterized by pushing her sons toward education
rather than fighting, and by her frustration with Goku's repeated absences.

--- YAMCHA ---

Yamcha begins the series as a desert bandit who robs travelers, alongside his shapeshifting companion
Puar. He later becomes an ally and trains under Master Roshi.

Yamcha's techniques include the Wolf Fang Fist, a rapid close-range strike combination, and the Spirit
Ball, a controllable energy sphere.

Yamcha is killed by a Saibaman self-destruct attack during the Saiyan saga. He becomes progressively
less relevant to combat as the power scale escalates, and pursues a career in professional baseball.

--- TIEN SHINHAN AND CHIAOTZU ---

Tien Shinhan is a martial artist trained by the Crane School, a rival to Master Roshi's Turtle School.
He has a third eye on his forehead. He begins as an antagonist in the Tenkaichi Budokai tournament arc
and later becomes a permanent ally.

Tien Shinhan's signature technique is the Tri-Beam, called the Kikoho in Japanese, a square-shaped
energy blast that damages the user's own body. He also created the Solar Flare, a blinding light
technique later adopted by Goku and Krillin.

Chiaotzu is Tien Shinhan's small, pale-faced companion and closest friend. Chiaotzu uses telepathy and
psychokinesis rather than raw physical power. He self-destructs in an attempt to kill Nappa during the
Saiyan saga.

--- MR. SATAN ---

Mr. Satan, known as Hercule in some English releases, is the reigning World Martial Arts Tournament
champion and a celebrity on Earth. He is dramatically weaker than the main cast but is publicly
credited with their victories.

Mr. Satan is the father of Videl and the grandfather of Pan. Despite being a fraud in terms of power
level, he plays a genuinely decisive role in the Buu saga by befriending Fat Buu and by rallying the
people of Earth to donate energy for Goku's Spirit Bomb.

--- ANDROID 18 ---

Android 18 is a human who was modified into a cyborg by Dr. Gero, alongside her twin brother Android
17. She was originally created as a weapon to kill Goku.

Android 18 is absorbed by Cell to reach his Perfect form and is later expelled when Gohan forces Cell
to regurgitate her.

Android 18 marries Krillin and they have a daughter named Marron. She becomes a member of the main
cast and competes in the Tournament of Power in Dragon Ball Super.

--- ANDROID 17 ---

Android 17 is the twin brother of Android 18 and was likewise converted by Dr. Gero. He is absorbed by
Cell and later restored when Cell is defeated.

Android 17 works as a park ranger on Earth in Dragon Ball Super, having married and taken in children.
He competes in the Tournament of Power representing Universe 7 and plays a decisive role in its
conclusion.

SECTION 3: DEITIES, GUARDIANS, AND NAMEKIANS
=========================================================================

--- KAMI ---

Kami is the guardian of Earth and the creator of Earth's Dragon Balls. He is the good half of an
original Namekian who split himself in two, with the evil half becoming King Piccolo.

Kami resides in the Lookout, a floating platform high above Earth, attended by Mr. Popo. Because Kami
and Piccolo are linked, the death of one kills the other, and the death of Kami also renders Earth's
Dragon Balls inert. Kami eventually fuses back with Piccolo.

--- DENDE ---

Dende is a young Namekian introduced on Planet Namek, where he helps Gohan and Krillin. He has healing
abilities characteristic of the Namekian healer class.

Dende becomes the new guardian of Earth after Kami fuses with Piccolo, and creates a new set of Earth
Dragon Balls.

--- KING KAI ---

King Kai, or North Kai, is a deity who lives on a tiny planet at the end of Snake Way in the afterlife.
He trains Goku after Goku's death against Raditz.

King Kai teaches Goku the Kaio-ken, a technique that multiplies the user's power at the cost of strain
on the body, and the Spirit Bomb. King Kai is also a comedic character fond of bad jokes.

--- KORIN AND YAJIROBE ---

Korin is a talking cat who lives atop Korin Tower and grows senzu beans, which fully restore a fighter's
health and energy when eaten. Senzu beans are a recurring plot device throughout the series.

Yajirobe is a samurai-styled character who lives with Korin. He is largely comedic but severs Great Ape
Vegeta's tail during the Saiyan saga, which is critical to that battle's outcome.

--- BEERUS ---

Beerus is the God of Destruction of Universe 7 and a central figure introduced in Dragon Ball Z: Battle
of Gods and Dragon Ball Super. His role is to destroy planets to maintain cosmic balance, complementing
the Supreme Kai's role of creation.

Beerus is linked to the Supreme Kai of Universe 7, meaning that if one dies the other dies as well.
Beerus is a cat-like being characterized by his short temper and his obsession with food.

--- WHIS ---

Whis is an angel and the attendant of Beerus. He is significantly more powerful than Beerus and serves
as his martial arts teacher.

Whis later trains Goku and Vegeta at Beerus's planet. Whis can manipulate time to a limited degree,
rewinding events by a few minutes, and can travel between planets carrying others.

SECTION 4: MAJOR ANTAGONISTS
=========================================================================

--- RADITZ ---

Raditz is Goku's older brother and the first villain of Dragon Ball Z. He arrives on Earth and reveals
Goku's Saiyan heritage and original mission.

Raditz is killed when Piccolo fires a Special Beam Cannon through both Raditz and Goku, who holds his
brother in place. This sacrifice sets up Goku's afterlife training with King Kai.

--- NAPPA ---

Nappa is a Saiyan warrior who arrives on Earth with Vegeta. He is responsible for the deaths of several
Z Fighters, including Chiaotzu, Yamcha, Tien Shinhan, and Piccolo during the Saiyan saga.

Nappa is killed by Vegeta after being crippled by Goku, discarded as useless.

--- FRIEZA ---

Frieza is a galactic emperor and one of the most significant antagonists in the franchise. He is
responsible for the destruction of Planet Vegeta and the near-extinction of the Saiyan race, fearing
the legend of the Super Saiyan.

Frieza has multiple transformations, described in the series as four primary forms, with his final form
being the sleek white and purple appearance most associated with him. His signature techniques include
the Death Beam and the Supernova.

Frieza is defeated by Goku on Namek in the fight that produces Goku's first Super Saiyan transformation.
He survives, is rebuilt as Mecha Frieza, and is then killed on Earth by Future Trunks.

Frieza is revived in Dragon Ball Super and achieves a form called Golden Frieza through training. In the
Dragon Ball Super manga he later attains a form called Black Frieza.

--- THE GINYU FORCE ---

The Ginyu Force is an elite mercenary squad serving Frieza, known for posing and theatrics. Its members
are Captain Ginyu, Recoome, Burter, Jeice, and Guldo.

Captain Ginyu's signature ability is a body-swapping technique that lets him trade bodies with an
opponent, which he uses on Goku before ending up trapped in the body of a frog.

--- DR. GERO AND THE ANDROIDS ---

Dr. Gero is a scientist of the Red Ribbon Army who creates a series of androids to take revenge on Goku
for destroying the organization years earlier. He converts himself into Android 20.

Dr. Gero is killed by Android 17, one of his own creations, after 17 and 18 are activated and refuse to
follow his orders.

--- CELL ---

Cell is a bio-android created by Dr. Gero's supercomputer from the cells of multiple fighters,
including Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo, and Frieza. He travels back in time from a future timeline in an
attempt to reach his complete form.

Cell progresses through three stages: Imperfect Cell, Semi-Perfect Cell after absorbing Android 17, and
Perfect Cell after absorbing Android 18.

Cell announces the Cell Games, a tournament staged to determine the fate of Earth. He is ultimately
destroyed by Gohan in Super Saiyan 2 form during a Kamehameha struggle, with Goku's spirit assisting.

--- MAJIN BUU ---

Majin Buu is an ancient creature of destruction sealed away long before the events of the series, and
awakened by the wizard Babidi and his father Bibidi's legacy.

Majin Buu appears in several forms across the arc. The initial childlike round form is commonly called
Fat Buu or Innocent Buu. After separating from his good half he becomes Evil Buu and then Super Buu.
His original, purest form is Kid Buu.

Fat Buu eventually becomes a friendly member of Earth's cast, befriended by Mr. Satan, and is known as
Mr. Buu or Good Buu.

Kid Buu is destroyed by a Spirit Bomb powered by energy from the people of Earth, gathered after Mr.
Satan appeals to them.

--- BABIDI AND DABURA ---

Babidi is a wizard who seeks to revive Majin Buu, continuing the work of his father Bibidi. He controls
followers using a mind-control technique that amplifies their power by drawing out their inner evil.

Dabura is the King of the Demon Realm and Babidi's most powerful servant. His spit turns victims to
stone.

--- BROLY ---

Broly is a Saiyan character who appears in both non-canon films and in the canonical Dragon Ball Super:
Broly. In the Dragon Ball Super version he is the son of Paragus and was exiled as an infant to the
harsh planet Vampa by King Vegeta.

Broly in the Dragon Ball Super version is portrayed as gentle in temperament but possessing enormous
uncontrolled power that surfaces as rage. He fights Goku and Vegeta and later becomes a reluctant ally.

--- GOKU BLACK AND ZAMASU ---

Zamasu is a Kai apprentice from Universe 10 who develops contempt for mortals and plots to eradicate
them, a plan referred to as the Zero Mortals Plan.

Goku Black is Zamasu from an alternate timeline who used the Super Dragon Balls to swap bodies with
Goku. He appears in the Future Trunks arc of Dragon Ball Super.

Zamasu and Goku Black fuse using Potara earrings to become Fused Zamasu, an immortal opponent that
Goku, Vegeta, and Future Trunks struggle to defeat.

--- JIREN ---

Jiren is a fighter from Universe 11 and a member of the Pride Troopers. He is the strongest competitor
in the Tournament of Power arc of Dragon Ball Super.

Jiren's power is described as exceeding that of a God of Destruction. His fight with Goku is the arc in
which Goku first achieves Ultra Instinct.

SECTION 5: NEXT GENERATION AND FUSIONS
=========================================================================

--- FUTURE TRUNKS ---

Future Trunks is the son of Vegeta and Bulma from an alternate timeline in which the Androids devastated
Earth and killed nearly all the Z Fighters.

Future Trunks travels to the past and kills Frieza and King Cold on their arrival at Earth, then warns
Goku about the Androids and about the heart virus that killed Goku in his timeline.

Future Trunks was trained by Future Gohan in his own timeline. He uses a sword and can transform into a
Super Saiyan. He returns later in Dragon Ball Super during the Goku Black arc.

--- TRUNKS ---

Trunks is the present-timeline son of Vegeta and Bulma, introduced as an infant during the Cell saga and
appearing as a child in the Buu saga. He can transform into a Super Saiyan at a very young age.

Trunks is the close friend and fusion partner of Goten.

--- GOTEN ---

Goten is the younger son of Goku and Chi-Chi, born after Goku's death in the Cell Games. He strongly
resembles his father as a child.

Goten learns the Fusion Dance with Trunks during the Buu saga, forming Gotenks.

--- GOTENKS ---

Gotenks is the fusion of Goten and Trunks via the Fusion Dance, a technique taught by Goku after he
learned it in the afterlife. The fusion lasts a limited time, roughly thirty minutes, and is shortened
further when the fused form transforms.

Gotenks is arrogant and prone to showing off, which repeatedly wastes his time limit during the Buu
saga. He can reach Super Saiyan 3.

--- VEGITO ---

Vegito is the fusion of Goku and Vegeta using the Potara earrings of the Supreme Kai. Potara fusion is
initially presented as permanent, though it is later established to have a time limit for mortals.

Vegito is created during the Buu saga to fight Super Buu, and appears in Dragon Ball Super as Vegito
Blue during the Goku Black arc.

--- GOGETA ---

Gogeta is the fusion of Goku and Vegeta using the Fusion Dance rather than the Potara earrings. He
appears in films including Dragon Ball Super: Broly, where he fights Broly as Gogeta Blue.

--- VIDEL AND PAN ---

Videl is the daughter of Mr. Satan and a skilled martial artist in her own right by human standards. She
learns to fly from Gohan and later marries him.

Pan is the daughter of Gohan and Videl, making her a quarter Saiyan. She appears as an infant at the end
of Dragon Ball Z and as a child in Dragon Ball GT and Dragon Ball Super: Super Hero.

=========================================================================
SECTION 6: UNIVERSE 6 SAIYANS AND TOURNAMENT CHARACTERS
=========================================================================

--- CABBA ---

Cabba is a Saiyan from Universe 6, introduced during the Universe 6 versus Universe 7 tournament arc of
Dragon Ball Super. Unlike Universe 7 Saiyans, the Universe 6 Saiyans are protectors rather than
conquerors.

Cabba is taught to transform into a Super Saiyan by Vegeta, who becomes something of a mentor to him.

--- CAULIFLA AND KALE ---

Caulifla is a Saiyan from Universe 6 who leads a gang and learns to become a Super Saiyan under Cabba's
instruction. Kale is her close friend and protege, whose rage-driven transformation resembles the
Legendary Super Saiyan state.

Caulifla and Kale fuse using Potara earrings to become Kefla during the Tournament of Power.

--- HIT ---

Hit is an assassin from Universe 6 with the ability to skip through time, initially by fractions of a
second, allowing him to strike before opponents can react. He fights Goku in both the Universe 6
tournament and the Tournament of Power.

SECTION 7: KEY CONCEPTS AND TERMINOLOGY
=========================================================================

--- DRAGON BALLS ---

The Dragon Balls are seven orange spheres marked with red stars, numbered one through seven. When all
seven are gathered, they summon a dragon who grants wishes. After use, the balls scatter across the
planet and turn to stone for a period of time.

Earth's Dragon Balls summon Shenron. Namek's Dragon Balls are much larger and summon Porunga, who grants
three wishes rather than one. The Super Dragon Balls are planet-sized and exist across universes.

--- KI ---

Ki is the life energy that fighters in Dragon Ball manipulate for attacks, flight, and defense. Sensing
ki allows characters to locate others without technology, which eventually makes Frieza's scouters
obsolete.

--- SUPER SAIYAN ---

Super Saiyan is a transformation available to Saiyans that dramatically increases power, turning the
hair golden and the eyes teal. It is described in-universe as a legend that appears once every thousand
years, and Goku is the first to achieve it in the series' present day.

Later stages include Super Saiyan 2, Super Saiyan 3, and the divine forms Super Saiyan God and Super
Saiyan Blue. Great Ape is a separate transformation triggered when a Saiyan with an intact tail looks at
a full moon or equivalent blutz wave source.

--- THE HYPERBOLIC TIME CHAMBER ---

The Hyperbolic Time Chamber is a room in Kami's Lookout where one year passes inside for every day
outside. It is used for accelerated training during the Cell and Buu sagas. Its interior features
extreme temperatures, heavy gravity, and thin air.

--- SENZU BEANS ---

Senzu beans are grown by Korin and restore a fighter to full health and energy when eaten, including
healing severe injuries. They are a recurring means of resetting a fighter's condition mid-arc.
